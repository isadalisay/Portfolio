From the Air to the Sea

A cartographic portrait of how the Philippines moves, passenger traffic through its major air and sea transit hubs across Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao, built from Illustrator, ArcGIS Pro, and Philippine government transit data.

A Winner of the Bill Loy Award. I made this map because of my background as a Filipina. I was born and raised in the US but I would hear from my parents how they would take boats to get to school and in an archipelago of more than 7,000 islands, planes and ferries are how people stay connected. I wanted to show that movement in a way anyone could read at a glance.

Hydrology & Hiking Trails in Angeles National Forest

A 3D terrain visualization of the Tujunga Watershed using orthoimagery and hypsometric tinting to highlight elevation, water flow, and hiking routes across the San Gabriel Mountains. This was the first time I created a 3D visualization and I was inspired by the maps I would see at national parks which I tried to emulate with the labeling of hiking trails and topography. Learning about the mountains that were right in my backyard helped me understand the importance of these mountains as hubs for biodiversity, providing crucial water resources, and sources for wildfires.

Bike Share Site Suitability — Salem, OR

A weighted suitability analysis identifying optimal bike share station locations across Salem, combining population density and road network accessibility submodels into a single site recommendation. These maps were my introduction to model building in ArcGIS Pro and using urban data. I was asked a broad question of "Where should bikeshare racks be placed in a city?". I got to use my Python experience with the model builder, but the urban data I used such as building location, typography, and existing public transportation helped introduce me to spaitial analysis and location allocation analysis in an urban setting.

ArcGIS ProSite SuitabilitySpatial Analysis

LA Grocery Stores

An interactive Plotly Dash dashboard comparing grocery store locations across Los Angeles County against city-level census data, built to explore food access alongside demographic patterns. Before I made the scrolly-telling map above I created this dashboard to compare grocery store locations with Census data in Los Angeles. I wanted to see if the placements of these grocery stores are purposeful and whether certain communities are underserved despite the number of grocery stores in Los Angeles.

Atlas of Wyoming — Draft Maps

Draft maps I produced at the InfoGraphics Lab for the Atlas of Wyoming, visualizing elk and pronghorn migration around Cody and the Absaroka Front: migration corridors and stopovers, an individual animal's seasonal route and elevation profile, disease transmission, fence modifications, and where the two herds' ranges overlap. Working at the lab gave me the chance to work with government data and wildlife migration data. I also learned the importance of understanding one's audience as the atlas will eventually be published. Each is watermarked as a draft.
